      Cake care and pickup

      A few simple tips so your order reaches the table looking and tasting its best.

      Pickup

      Pickup is at Mayfield Park Residences. Once your order is confirmed, we message you to agree on the day and time.

      Please come at the time we agreed. Message us when you arrive, and have a quick look at your order before you leave.

      Delivery by rider

      If you choose delivery, we book a Lalamove or Grab rider for you and the rider fee is paid by you. Please be ready to receive the cake at the agreed time, and ask the rider to keep the box flat and level.

      Getting your cake home

      Keep the box flat at all times, on the floor of the car or on your lap, never tilted or on a sloped seat. Carry it from the bottom with both hands, don't stack anything on top, and keep the air conditioning on if it's warm.

      Keeping it fresh

      Keep your cake in the fridge until you're ready to serve it, and away from direct sun and heat. Take it out about 30 minutes before serving so it's soft and full of flavour. Cover leftovers and return them to the fridge, and enjoy them within 2 to 3 days.

      When you cut, use a clean, warm knife and wipe it between slices for neat edges.

      Pastries

      Keep brownies, cookies, ensaymada and cupcakes in an airtight box, away from heat, and enjoy them within a day or two. Anything with cream or frosting is best kept in the fridge.

      Allergies

      Please tell us about any allergies in the notes when you order, so we can talk it through before we bake.

      Questions

      If anything about your order isn't right, message us as soon as you can using the details in the footer of this page.
