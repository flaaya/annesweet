How to order

  1. Send a request

    Choose from the menu or describe a custom design in the form below. Everything is baked fresh to order, so we need 3 days (a week for custom cakes).

  2. We message you

    We confirm the details and the price with you and arrange payment.

  3. Pick up or get it delivered

    Collect it from us, or we book a Lalamove or Grab rider for you. The rider fee is paid by the customer.
