      Privacy policy

      Last updated: November 2026

      This page explains what this website collects when you send an order request, why, and where it goes. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

      What the order form collects

      • Your name and mobile number
      • Your Facebook name, if you give it
      • Your delivery address, if you choose delivery
      • The date you need your order
      • The items and sizes you choose, your custom cake details and any notes

      We also record the date and time your request arrives.

      Why we collect it

      Only to confirm and fulfil your order: to message you about the final price and payment, to have your cakes ready on the right date, and to arrange pickup or delivery.

      Where it goes

      When you send the form, your details go to a Google Sheet owned by Annesweet, through Google Apps Script. An order alert with your details is also sent to the owner's Telegram account so we see new orders quickly. Google and Telegram store this data on their own servers under their own privacy policies.

      This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it receives your IP address when it sends you the pages.

      What stays on your device

      Items you tap Add on are kept in your browser's session storage until you close the tab, so they appear on the order form. Cakes you save with the heart are kept in your browser's local storage on your device until you remove them. Neither is sent to us.

      Fonts, cookies and tracking

      The fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads. We use no analytics and no advertising cookies.

      Your rights

      You can ask to see the details we hold about you, correct them or have them deleted. Contact us using the details in the footer of this page.
