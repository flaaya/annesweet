      Terms and conditions

      Last updated: November 2026

      These terms explain how ordering from Annesweet works. By sending an order request, you agree to them.

      Order requests

      Sending the form is a request, not a confirmed order. Your order is confirmed only when Annesweet messages you.

      Lead time

      Please order at least 3 days ahead, and at least a week ahead for custom cakes.

      Price and payment

      Prices on the site are a guide. The final price and how to pay are confirmed by message before we bake.

      Any deposit or payment needed is confirmed with each order.

      Pickup and delivery

      You can pick up your order from us, or we book a Lalamove or Grab rider for you. The rider fee is paid by you.

      Changes and cancellations

      If you need to change or cancel an order, contact Annesweet as early as possible.

      What can be changed, and any refund, depends on how far along your order is and is confirmed with each order.

      Ingredients and allergies

      Please tell us about any allergies in the notes when you order. Ingredient and allergen details are confirmed with each order.

      Photos

      Photos on this site are illustrations. Every cake is made by hand, so yours will look a little different.

      Contact

      For anything about your order, contact us using the details in the footer of this page.
